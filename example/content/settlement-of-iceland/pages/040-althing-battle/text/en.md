At the Althing of 1012 the killing of Njáll Þorgeirsson, burned in his house with his family the year
before, came to court. The case against Flosi Þórðarson and the burners failed on a point of procedure,
and fighting broke out on the assembly plain. Síðu-Hallr, whose son Ljótr was killed in the fight, gave up
the compensation due for him so that a settlement could be made.
